This app is run from local computer for voice based menu addition by vendors. And it also takes the load off vendor to calculate the total for each customer in peak times.
For Laptop
- Launch index.html from dist folder directly,via google chrome
- choose the lanaguage you prefer there are major regionnal language already build in 
- Add menu by saying or uploading the image in the app. 
- Setup is ready now proceed to let customer at counter calculate the total they have to pay

For Mobile
- Use the downloadable APK file directly, google app store is tough to roll app it cost more now
- App runs locally on your system
- data remains on your system you can export and use it for any purpose
- Latest release will be updated on the GitHub for newer featuers
- You have option to present this to customer or use your self for faster calculation in parallel to your primary billing system as needed
- Targetting very limited resources store and vendor which have only mobile phone at there store for handling customers .e.g. Pani Puri Vendors, fancy jewelry shops, etc.

FAQ
- Your menu your price and you can keep updating price 
- No tax option added, later stages will support GST in current form you can track if calculations are all matching or not
- Battery will not be consumed by this app alone
